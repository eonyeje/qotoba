Conventions

How these pages are written

Every document in this folder is a single HTML page on one shared stylesheet, light and dark, readable on a phone. Diagrams are inline SVG with a caption stating their claim, and every diagram is followed by the prose that carries the detail. Status chips mean the same thing on every page: built, in progress, designed, vision, gap. Figures quote their source and date, and estimates say what unit they are in.
