Qotoba Naming Decision

Qotoba is the public name of the project previously code-named Quanta 2.0, and before that Jargon and Dialect. The name was chosen after a screen of 53 candidates across package registries, GitHub, ten domain endings and the web.

Status: DecidedPronounced KO-to-baRepository: github.com/eonyeje/qotoba

Origin and pitch

Qotoba comes from the Japanese 言葉, kotoba, meaning "words" or "language". The Q replaces the K to mark the name as part of the Q family that the project adopted for its public identity. The meaning is the product: the developer writes words, in a modern dialect of a language they already think in, and Qotoba turns those words into the program.

The pitch

Your words become the program. A meta-language, an agentic coding toolchain and an IDE that understands your dialect.

Two readings of the name are both acceptable. English readers will say "KO-to-ba" or "ko-TOE-ba"; Japanese stress is flat, so neither is wrong. The README fixes "KO-to-ba" so that one form appears in talks and videos.

Availability at decision time

Every surface below was checked on 5 November 2026 by direct lookup: the registry APIs for packages, a page load for GitHub, and RDAP for domains (Verisign for .com, Google for .dev, Identity Digital for .io). Web searches found no company, product or live trademark named Qotoba. The only neighbours are unrelated open-source projects spelled "kotoba".

Namespace scheme

One name, one rule per surface. The bare name is the product, the CLI and the language. Packages are scoped so that every library sits under the same prefix. Domains point at one site.

Names considered and rejected

The screen started from the brief: an exotic, short name with a Q or X, built from African or Asian words, that says what the tool does. Three rounds narrowed it. The first round looked at single words and mutations, the second at three-letter codes, the third at the user's own top three.

QotobaJapanese kotoba, wordsChosen. Free everywhere except a parked .com; no company anywhere.
DialeqDialect with a QRunner-up. Clean, but reads as a misspelling and sits next to Dialex, Dialexa and Dialexia.
VernaqVernacular with a QOut. A live Irish software product with the same concept, registered March 2026.
DialexGreek dialexis, discourseOut. Three AI language products already use it; every major domain taken.
QambaZulu ukuqamba, to composeOut. Brazilian no-code company holds qamba.com; an AI video project squats the labs org.
QuambaQamba, English spellingOut. The name of a cited ML paper on quantised Mamba models.
JDQ, DJQ, QDJ…three-letter codesOut. All short domains squatted; QDJ has a live US trademark in the software class.
AnlaqAzerbaijani, understandingStrong alternate. Free everywhere including .com; not chosen on sound.

The full matrix for all 53 candidates is in the name screen.

Registrations to make

These are manual steps for the owner, in the order that protects the name best. None had been done at the time of this record.

  1. Create the GitHub org qotoba, then transfer eonyeje/qotoba into it.
  2. Register qotoba.dev, then qotoba.io and qotoba.ai.
  3. Publish a placeholder @qotoba/core on npm to hold the scope.
  4. Publish 0.0.1 stubs named qotoba on crates.io and PyPI.
  5. Register @qotoba on X and YouTube; check Reddit by hand.
  6. Set a drop watch on qotoba.com.
